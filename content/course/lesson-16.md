**The Power of Intention and Purpose**

![](https://www.personalgrowthcourses.net/images/intentions_purpose_path.jpg)

> *Whatever you can do, or dream you can do, begin it.
> Boldness has genius, power, and magic in it.
> Begin it now.*
>   ~  Goethe

Now that you've further explored the shift in paradigms of which we are all a part, we invite you to consider how this applies to your own life. **Possibly the **single** most important action transformation team members can take to help the shift to a new paradigm based on love and empowerment is to get clear on their purpose and intentions in life**, and to then live that purpose and follow those intentions to the very best of their ability.

Life purpose is a single statement of deep meaning about a person's reason for being. Life intentions provide direction for moving through specific challenges which can arise in any moment. Life purpose can be extremely useful for keeping ourselves on track in life from the larger perspective, while life intentions can give us direction at key choice points with which we are presented. For a fun, one-minute video demonstrating the power of intentions, click below:

[**https://www.personalgrowthcourses.net/video/intentional\_chocolate**](https://www.personalgrowthcourses.net/video/intentional_chocolate)

We invite all transformation team members to consider getting clear on their intentions and purpose in life. The reason for this is that those who are not clear can end up being swayed by outside influences. When people are not clear about what they want in life, they often follow the path of least resistance, which sometimes means following unconscious desires or other people's suggestions which may or may not be in their best interest.

**When we are clear on our deepest intentions and purpose, we can powerfully move through any challenges that might arise as we work together to create a new paradigm both for ourselves and our planet.**

Some of you have already done significant work on this, while others have yet to reach clarity. If you are already clear, we invite you to read through the guide at the link below as a review, and to open to new ideas which might help further refine what guides you through life.

For those who have little experience in this realm, we invite you to read this essay carefully and explore some of the many links provided so that you might become clear on both your deepest intentions and your greater purpose in this life.

[www.weboflove.org/lifepurposeintentions](https://www.weboflove.org/lifepurposeintentions) - Life Purpose and Intentions

**We highly recommend that all Transformation Team members develop written statements of life purpose and intentions, so that both individually and collectively we can move more powerfully in our world and inspire others by the clear principles on which we base our lives.**

At the end of this lesson, we will ask those registered for the course to write your life purpose and intentions along with your impressions of this material before moving on to the next lesson. Writing a statement like this can be a wonderful way of declaring yourself in the world and allowing others to offer you support and encouragement in living your purpose and intentions.

For those who join the community portal of our website, you will have a personal profile page describing you and your passions. Your life purpose and intentions will also be listed there. This allows our members to learn about these important aspects of each other, as well as providing a means for those with similar purpose and intentions to find and support each other, and to more powerfully work together to promote transformation in our lives and world.

**Take Action:** Suggested Activities for Transformation

- Write down your life intentions and purpose and post them where you can see them easily to be frequently reminded of what you really want in life. When you wake up in the morning and just before going to bed at night, remind and recommit yourself to your purpose and intentions.
- Open to guidance and suggestions from friends, family, and your spirit guides in refining and further developing your intentions. Ask for feedback and support in living the life you envision. For every challenge you face in life, you can develop an intention to guide you when the going gets rough.
- Consider breathing sacred love as one of your intentions and join us in strengthening the web of love which interconnects all beings.
- Notice when unconscious parts of yourself (thoughts, feelings, desires) arise to block you from following your deeper intentions and purpose. Acknowledge these parts and find a way to give them a voice. Engage in dialog with these conflicting parts of yourself, inviting them to harmonize with your deeper intentions. Invite spiritual guidance to help facilitate this dialog.
- What do you think the purpose and intentions of the collective consciousness of our planet might be? What would you like it to be? How coherent is this collective will? How do you relate to this? Considering that every one of us has an impact on this, explore how you might bring greater coherence and harmony to our collective consciousness.
- What do you think is the meaning of life? How is this related or not related to the purpose and intentions you have developed for yourself?
