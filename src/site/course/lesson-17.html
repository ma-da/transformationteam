Inspiration and Transformation: Keep It Simple

Energy flows where attention goes.

Though life for humanity on planet Earth and the challenges that come up in our individual lives can be infinitely complex, the principles behind what happens are often quite simple.

Once you get clear on your life purpose and life intentions, for instance, you may be surprised at how things begin to make more sense and fall into place much more easily than before. If you go deep down the rabbit hole of what is happening behind the scenes in our world and beyond, you can spend the rest of your life exploring all of the many facets and strange permutations of everything going on, yet the principles behind much of what is happening are not difficult to understand.

In this lesson, we summarize the basic principles presented in this course and introduce a couple inspiring essays and a powerful video presenting simple techniques for transformation. The first essay is titled Simple Keys to a Fuller Life. To read this simple, yet profound eight-page essay, click on the link below:

www.weboflove.org/keystolife - Simple Keys to a Fuller Life

For some principles which can help when we are in a stuck place with someone, the practice of nonviolent communication can be a simple, yet powerful way to break through the barriers we create with each other. In a 10-minute interview with the founder of Nonviolent Communication, Marshall Rosenberg discusses how he transformed some very tense situations with these simple techniques.

www.personalgrowthcourses.net/video… - Nonviolent Communication

The below six-page summary of Nonviolent Communication provides a brief, yet valuable background to help understand how you can use this simple, yet wonderful tool for transforming communication.

www.personalgrowthcourses.net/stories/nvc.nonviolent_communication

Nonviolent communication gives us another simple, yet powerful tool to be an effective agent of change and transformation in our lives and world. One purpose of this course is to provide you with a tool box filled with simple, effective tools you can use to more fully tap into your infinite potential. Below is a comprehensive list of simple principles presented in this and previous lessons of the Transformation Course which you can use as tools for transformation in your life:

Moment of Love

  • Every person in the world has a heart.
  • Every heart has a place within that wants only to love and be loved.

W. Mitchell video

  • It’s not what happens to you that matters, it’s what you do about it.

Ho’oponopono healing technique
- I love you - I’m sorry - Please forgive me - Thank you - We love you - We’re sorry - Please forgive us - Thank you all

Web of Love

  • Your sacred love flows in to me.
  • My sacred love flows out to you.

Creating a New Paradigm

  • Develop purpose and intentions for your life.
  • Transform fear into acceptance and love.
  • Take personal responsibility for your life.
  • Avoid secrecy and encourage openness and transparency.
  • Transcend polarization, ‘good vs. evil’ and ‘us vs. them’ beliefs.
  • Join groups and communities to support each other in all this.

Principles of Transformation

  • Seeing the Divine in all
  • Nurturance of life
  • Gratitude

Simple Keys to A Fuller Life

  • What’s Best for All
  • Divine Guidance
  • Acceptance and Understanding
  • Love and Empowerment

Nonviolent Communication

  • State concrete actions you observe in yourself or the other person.
  • State your feelings or guess the other person’s feelings about this.
  • State the need behind your feeling or guess the other person’s need.
  • Make a concrete request for action to meet the need identified.

We recommend that you print these simple principles and review them from time to time along with your life purpose and intentions. For a one-page print version of the above, click here. When the complexities of life and the world seem overwhelming or major challenges rise to greet us, we can come back to our life purpose and intentions and to the above simple principles as a means of staying on a clear path to transformation even in the midst of the greatest storms.

Take Action: Suggested Activities for Transformation

  • Print the one-page summary of simple principles available on the first page of this lesson. Select one or more of these sets of principles to play with. Explore how you can use these inspiring tools to support your purpose and intentions, and to powerfully transform your life and our world for the better.
  • Notice when you get caught up in the complexities of a particular relationship or situation. Remember then to stop and take a breath. Let go of the complexities for a moment. Take a deep breath with everyone involved. Then remind yourself and all involved of the simple principles and intentions underlying what you all really want in the challenge you are facing. Invite everyone to remember their needs and their shared deeper intentions.
  • Be aware of when you are choosing to overly focus on the negative things happening in our world. Set an intention that when you catch yourself going there again, you will choose to shift and open to inspiration in some way.
  • Develop a joy meditation where you open to the love and joy that is always there within. Consider using a powerfully joyful or moving experience from your past as an anchor to bring you back to this beautiful state. Be brave in following your passion in life.
  • Make a commitment to send a mental or psychic hello and to breathe sacred love with people you come across during your day. Remind yourself frequently of your intention to feel more connected and more in love with all around you.
  • Sign up for an inspirational news service or magazine to bring more joy, hope, and inspiration into your life. See below for ideas.
