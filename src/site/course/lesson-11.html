Reminder: The information in the Transformation Course has the potential to create big shifts in our lives. Yet when we open to profound changes, life does not necessarily always become easier right away. Sometimes things get more difficult or even chaotic before we make a significant breakthrough. Along with the inspiring new changes and realizations we embrace, big challenges can arise which in some way test us or invite us to take what we learn even further.

Don’t be surprised during your journey through the course if unusual things or strange “coincidences” start happening in your life, both challenging and inspirational. If difficult challenges arise, look for the lesson wanting to be learned and open to guidance and support as you remember the unlimited potential inside you. If magical serendipity enters your life, celebrate and enjoy it! You will likely experience some of both, with much more on the inspirational side.

Don’t hesitate to send a message any time you might want some support or to share some wonderful breakthroughs. Our guides enjoy hearing from you and sharing thoughts and ideas. In lesson 12, we explore the sources of infinite energy available to each of us and to our world. Have a beautiful day!
