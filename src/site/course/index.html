Transformation Course

The 21-Lesson Transformation Course

The free Transformation Course invites us to explore personal and planetary transformation through love and empowerment. Each lesson offers thought-provoking material for reflection and action, and generally takes one to two hours to complete.

New to the course? Start with the Cosmic Fable and Fluid Intelligence, or read the course foreword before Lesson 1.
